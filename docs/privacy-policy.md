# Omnicore Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Omnicore is a desktop application that publishes the **AI, Actually** channel's own videos to its social media accounts through official platform APIs.

## What Omnicore accesses
When an account is connected with TikTok, Omnicore receives:
- basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), used to confirm it is connected to the right account;
- access and refresh tokens, used to upload videos the operator has approved and to check their publishing status.

## Where it is stored
Everything stays on the operator's own computer. Tokens and app credentials are encrypted with the Windows data-protection key store. Omnicore has no servers, does not sell or share data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising or analytics.

## Other people's data
Omnicore does not collect information about viewers or other TikTok users.

## Retention and deletion
Disconnecting the account in Omnicore deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).
