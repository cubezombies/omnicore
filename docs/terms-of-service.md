# Omnicore Terms of Service

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Omnicore is a desktop application operated for the **AI, Actually** channel. It produces short AI and technology explainer videos and, after a person reviews and approves each one, posts them to the channel's own social media accounts through official platform APIs (such as TikTok's Content Posting API).

## Who can use it
Omnicore is used only by the operator of AI, Actually, to manage that channel's own accounts. It is not offered as a service to the public, and it does not post to anyone else's account.

## Connecting a TikTok account
When you sign in with TikTok, you authorize Omnicore to read basic profile information and publish videos to your account. Omnicore posts only content you have approved, and only to the account you connected. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).

## Content
- All videos are labelled as AI-generated where the platform supports it.
- Videos use only music, images and other material that is licensed for that use or created by Omnicore.
- You remain responsible for content posted to your account and for following each platform's terms and community guidelines.
