# Omnicore

Omnicore is the desktop studio behind **AI, Actually**, a short-video channel explaining AI and technology news.

It researches stories from cited sources, produces short vertical videos with clearly labelled AI narration, and checks each one for accuracy and licensing. A person previews and approves every video before Omnicore posts it to the channel's own accounts through official platform APIs, such as TikTok's Content Posting API.
